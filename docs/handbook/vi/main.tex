\section{Danh gia dieu hanh}
\subsection{Muc dich kinh doanh}
San pham bien feedback van ban thanh tin hieu van hanh co cau truc: sentiment tong the, sentiment theo aspect, dau hieu sarcasm, va giai thich muc token. Gia tri kinh doanh nam o phan tich review, theo doi chat luong dich vu, ho tro triage support, va demo explainable AI.

\subsection{Muc dich san pham}
Repository dang dong thoi chua hai san pham:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
  \item dich vu inference online qua Angular va FastAPI
  \item he thong ML offline cho preprocessing, finetuning, evaluation, MLflow, va ONNX export
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Muc dich ky thuat}
Codebase the hien duoc day du chuoi phan phoi ML:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
  \item DVC quan ly data flow
  \item Hugging Face va PEFT cho training
  \item ONNX runtime cho serving
  \item Prometheus va Grafana cho observability
  \item Docker Compose cho dong goi va chay he thong
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Gia tri cot loi}
Gia tri chinh khong nam o model moi la. Gia tri nam o viec dong goi sentiment inference thanh mot dich vu co explainability, batch processing, monitoring, va data/model lineage ro rang.

\subsection{Danh gia do truong thanh}
\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lX@{}}

\subsection{Vi sao chua san sang production}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
  \item \texttt{src/main.py} mo toan bo endpoint inference va evaluation ma khong co auth.
  \item \texttt{/batch\_status/\{job\_id\}} la mock, khong co queue hay persistent state.
  \item frontend cho phep chon ngon ngu nhung \texttt{SentimentAnalysisService.predict()} bo qua truong \texttt{lang}.
  \item Grafana duoc khoi tao voi mat khau mac dinh \texttt{admin}.
  \item health check chi xac nhan model da load, khong xac minh toan bo dependency xuong duoi.
\end{itemize}

\section{CTO review}
\subsection{Co phe duyet production launch khong?}
Khong, neu la production mo hoac enterprise production. Co the phe duyet demo noi bo va pilot rat kiem soat sau khi hardening bao mat co ban.

\subsection{Co phe duyet enterprise customers khong?}
Khong. Security va operations controls hien tai chua dat muc toi thieu.

\subsection{Co phe duyet scaling khong?}
Chua. Kien truc co huong scale duoc, nhung deployment va control plane hien tai chua san sang.

\subsection{Dieu dang lo nhat la gi?}
Nguy hiem nhat la \emph{false confidence}: repo nhin rat giong san pham production, nhung mot so control quan trong van thieu hoac chi la mock.

\subsection{Nen dau tu tiep vao dau?}
Uu tien hardening nen tang: authentication, rate limiting, artifact governance, va durable batch execution. Sau do moi dau tu manh hon cho multilingual va model operations.
